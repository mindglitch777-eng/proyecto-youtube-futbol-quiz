'''Wayne Mark Rooney''' (born 24 October 1985) is an English former professional [[Association football|football]] player and [[Manager (association football)|manager]]. Widely regarded as one of the best players of his generation and one of the greatest players in [[Premier League]] history, Rooney won five league titles and remains the only player to have recorded both [[List of footballers with 100 or more Premier League goals|200 goals]] and [[Premier League records and statistics#Assists|100 assists]] in the competition.<ref>{{Cite web |date=7 June 2022 |title=Wayne Rooney Has Been Named The Greatest British Player Of All Time |url=https://www.sportbible.com/football/wayne-rooney-has-been-named-the-greatest-british-player-of-all-time-20220607 |access-date=23 August 2024 |website=SPORTbible }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Stevenson |first=William |date=17 August 2024 |title=12 Most 'Complete' Players in Football History [Ranked] |url=https://www.givemesport.com/most-complete-players-football-history-ranked/ |access-date=4 February 2025 |website=GiveMeSport }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Jiang |first=Allan |title=Wayne Rooney and the Top 10 Premier League Players of All-Time |url=https://bleacherreport.com/articles/845582-wayne-rooney-and-the-top-10-premier-league-players-of-all-time |access-date=2026-07-17 |website=bleacherreport.com |language=en}}</ref> He spent most of his playing career as a [[Forward (association football)|forward]] but was also used in various [[Midfielder|midfield]] roles. He is [[Manchester United F.C.|Manchester United]]'s [[List of Manchester United F.C. records and statistics#Goalscorers|top goalscorer of all time]] with 253 goals and was the all-time English top goalscorer in the [[UEFA Champions League]] (34) from 2011 to 2024.<ref>{{Cite web |title=All-time Player &#124; Top goalscorers Stats &#124; UEFA Champions League |url=https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/history/rankings/players/goals_scored/ |access-date=3 August 2025 |website=UEFA.com }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=17 September 2024 |title=Harry Kane passes Wayne Rooney as records fall in Bayern Munich rout |url=https://www.espn.co.uk/football/story/_/id/41303945/kane-passes-rooney-records-fall-bayern-munich-rout#:~:text=Harry%20Kane%20passes%20Wayne%20Rooney%20as%20records%20fall%20in%20Bayern%20Munich%20rout,-ESPN%20News%20Services&text=MUNICH%20%2D%2D%20Harry%20Kane%20scored,English%20player%20in%20the%20competition. |access-date=17 July 2026 |website=ESPN}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=21 October 2011 |title=Manchester United`s Wayne Rooney becomes highest-scoring English player in Champions League history after brace against Otelul Galati |url=https://azertag.az/en/xeber/manchester_uniteds_wayne_rooney_becomes_highest_scoring_english_player_in_champions_league_history_after_brace_against_otelul_galati-284518 |access-date=17 July 2026 |website=Azertag}}</ref> Rooney was named the [[FIFPRO#FIFPRO Young Player of the Year (2005–2008)|FIFPRO Young Player of the Year]] in 2004–05, selected in the [[ESM Team of the Season]] for 2009–10, and included in the [[2011 FIFA Ballon d'Or#FIFA/FIFPro World XI|2011 FIFA
