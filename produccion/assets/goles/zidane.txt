'''Zinedine Yazid Zidane'''{{efn-ua|Zidane's first name ''Zinedine'' is also written as '''Zinédine''' in French context.<ref name="FFF">{{Cite web|url=https://www.fff.fr/equipe-nationale/joueur/9772-zidane-zinedine/fiche.html|title=Zinédine Zidane|publisher=[[French Football Federation]]|access-date=30 January 2026|language=fr|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260217154108/https://www.fff.fr/equipe-nationale/joueur/9772-zidane-zinedine/fiche.html|archive-date=17 February 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="Decret2008"/>}} ({{IPA|fr|zinedin zidan|lang}};<ref>{{Cite web |date=23 June 2022|title=Zinédine Zidane, une vie: l'interview XXL avec la légende des Bleus |trans-title=Zinedine Zidane, a life: the XXL interview with the legend of the Blues |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o-Q2-VTopds |access-date=1 June 2025|website=[[YouTube]] |publisher=Téléfoot |language=French}}</ref> born 23 June 1972), popularly known as '''Zizou''' ({{IPA|fr|zizu|lang}}), is a French professional [[Manager (association football)|football manager]] and former [[Football player|player]] who played as an [[attacking midfielder]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://actas.rfef.es/actas/RFEF_CmpActa1?cod_primaria=1000144&CodActa=57218 |title=Acta del Partido celebrado el 12 de mayo de 2019, en San Sebastián-Donostia |trans-title=Minutes of the Match held on 12 May 2019, in San Sebastián-Donostia |publisher=Royal Spanish Football Federation |access-date=14 June 2019|language=es |archive-date=14 December 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191214051813/http://actas.rfef.es/actas/RFEF_CmpActa1?cod_primaria=1000144&CodActa=57218 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Since July 2026, he is the head coach of the [[France national football team|France national team]]. 

Widely regarded as one of the greatest players of all time, Zidane was a [[playmaker]] renowned for his elegance, vision, passing, and ball control. He received many individual accolades as a player, including being named [[FIFA World Player of the Year]] in 1998, 2000 and 2003, and winning the 1998 [[Ballon d'Or]]. He coached his former club [[Real Madrid CF|Real Madrid]] in two separate stints between 2016 and 2021, winning multiple domestic and international club trophies.

Zidane started his career at [[AS Cannes|Cannes]] before establishing himself as one of the best players in the French [[Ligue 1]] at [[FC Girondins de Bordeaux|Bordeaux]]. In 1996, he moved to Italian club [[Juventus FC|Juventus]], where he won two [[Serie A]] titles and played in consecutive [[UEFA Champions League]] finals in [[1997 UEFA Champions League final|1997]] and [[1998 UEFA Champions League final|1998]]. He moved to Real Madrid for a [[List of most expensive association football transfers|world-record fee]] at the time of €77.5&nbsp;million in 2001, which remained a record for the next eight years. In Spain, Zidane won several trophies, including a La Liga title and the Champions League.
