'''Vinícius José Paixão de Oliveira Júnior''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|viˈnisjuz ʒoˈzɛ pajˈʃɐ̃w̃ dʒi oliˈvejɾɐ ˈʒunjoʁ|lang}}; born 12 July 2000), known [[Mononym|mononymously]] as '''Vinícius''', is a Brazilian professional [[Association football|footballer]] who plays as a [[Forward (association football)|forward]] for {{Spanish football updater|Real Madrid}} club [[Real Madrid CF|Real Madrid]] and the [[Brazil national football team|Brazil national team]].

Born in [[São Gonçalo, Rio de Janeiro|São Gonçalo]], Vinícius began his professional career at [[Clube de Regatas do Flamengo|Flamengo]], where he made his senior debut in 2017, at age 16. A few weeks later, Vinícius was the subject of a transfer to La Liga club Real Madrid, for whom he signed in a £38 million deal, which was a national record for a player under 18.<ref>{{cite news |title=Madrid present £38.7m teenager Vinicius |publisher=BBC Sport |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/football/44904288 |date=20 July 2018 |access-date=27 May 2022 |archive-date=8 November 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221108213611/https://www.bbc.com/sport/football/44904288 |url-status=live }}</ref> The transfer was made effective after his 18th birthday, with Vinícius debuting for the club in [[2018–19 Real Madrid CF season|2018–19]]. Over the following seasons, Vinícius established himself as a prominent member in Real Madrid's squad, helping the club win two La Liga–[[UEFA Champions League|Champions League]] doubles, scoring the winning goal in the [[2022 UEFA Champions League final|2022 final]], as well as the second goal in the [[2024 UEFA Champions League final|2024 final]], being named [[UEFA Champions League#Awards|the tournament's Player of the Season]] in 2024.<ref>{{cite web |author1=Ed McCambridge Contributions from Mark White |date=18 March 2022 |title=Ranked! The 10 best wingers in the world right now |url=https://www.fourfourtwo.com/features/ranked-the-10-best-wingers-in-the-world-right-now |access-date=27 May 2022 |website=FourFourTwo |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230926055937/https://www.fourfourtwo.com/features/ranked-the-10-best-wingers-in-the-world-right-now |archive-date=26 September 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/0275-15415a62aaf0-36d3e17d7404-1000/ |title=Champions League final: Vinícius Júnior scores only goal as Real Madrid beat Liverpool to claim 14th title |publisher=UEFA |date=28 May 2022 |access-date=28 May 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240402222733/https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/0275-15415a62aaf0-36d3e17d7404-1000/ |archive-date=2 April 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite we
