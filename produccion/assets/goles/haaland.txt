'''Erling Braut Haaland'''{{efn|group=upper-alpha|Also spelled '''Erling Braut Håland'''}} (born 21 July 2000) is a <!--Do not add English- or British-born. Per MOS:NATIONALITY, unless ethnicity contributes to notability, it should not be mentioned in the opening. Please use the talk page to discuss notability before adding.-->Norwegian professional [[Association football|footballer]] who plays as a [[Striker (association football)|striker]] for {{English football updater|ManchesC}} club [[Manchester City F.C.|Manchester City]] and the [[Norway national football team|Norway national team]]. Widely regarded as one of the best players in the world and the greatest Norwegian player of all time, he is known for his speed, strength, positioning, and finishing ability, especially from inside the [[penalty area]].

Haaland began his senior career with Norwegian clubs [[Bryne FK|Bryne]] and [[Molde FK|Molde]] before signing in 2019 for [[FC Red Bull Salzburg|Red Bull Salzburg]], where he won the [[Austrian Football Bundesliga|Austrian Bundesliga]] and [[Austrian Cup]]. His performances in the [[UEFA Champions League]] earned him a move in 2020 to [[Bundesliga]] club [[Borussia Dortmund]], where he won the [[DFB-Pokal]] in [[2020–21 DFB-Pokal|2021]] and was named Bundesliga Player of the Season. In 2022, Haaland joined Manchester City for a fee of €60 million (£51.2 million) and played a pivotal role in the club's [[Treble (association football)|treble]]-winning debut season, becoming only the second English club to achieve the feat. He finished as the top scorer in the Premier League, [[FA Cup]] and Champions League, setting records for the [[Premier League records and statistics#Goalscoring|most goals in a Premier League season]] (36) and the most goals by a Premier League player in all competitions in a single season (52). At City, he has won two Premier League titles and three [[Premier League Golden Boot]]s.

Haaland's individual honours include the [[PFA Players' Player of the Year]], the [[UEFA Men's Player of the Year Award|UEFA Men's Player of the Year]], the [[IFFHS World's Best Player]] and the [[Golden Boy (award)|Golden Boy]] awards, while he also finished runner-up for the [[Ballon d'Or]]. He is the only player to win the awards for [[Premier League]] [[Premier League Young Player of the Season|Young Player]] and [[Premier League Player of the Season|Player of the Season]] in the same season. He is also the quickest Premier League player to [[List of Premier League hat-tricks|score eight hat-tricks]]. He was included in the [[FIFPRO World 11]] for 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2026.
