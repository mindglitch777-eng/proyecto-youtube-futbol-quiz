'''Ronaldo Luís Nazário de Lima''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ʁoˈnawdu luˈiz‿naˈzaɾju dʒi ˈlimɐ|lang}}; born 18 September 1976),<ref name="birthdate"/> [[mononym]]ously known as '''Ronaldo''', is a Brazilian former professional [[Association football|footballer]] who played as a [[striker (association football)|striker]]. He is the former owner and president of [[Segunda División]] club [[Real Valladolid]]. Nicknamed '''O Fenômeno''' and '''R9''', he is widely regarded as one of the greatest players of all time.<ref>{{cite web |url =https://www.nytimes.com/2014/06/11/sports/soccer/on-team-of-all-time-greats-pele-shines-brightest.html |title =On Team of All-Time Greats, Pelé Shines Brightest |author =Rob Hughes |newspaper=[[The New York Times]] |date =10 June 2014 |accessdate = 18 July 2025 |archiveurl =https://web.archive.org/web/20200726135158/https://www.nytimes.com/2014/06/11/sports/soccer/on-team-of-all-time-greats-pele-shines-brightest.html |archivedate =26 July 2020}}</ref> As a multi-functional striker who brought a new dimension to the position, Ronaldo has been an influence for the following generation of strikers. His individual accolades include being named [[FIFA World Player of the Year]] three times and winning two [[Ballon d'Or]] awards.

Ronaldo started his career at [[Cruzeiro Esporte Clube|Cruzeiro]]
