'''Neymar da Silva Santos Júnior'''{{efn|group=lower-greek|{{IPA|pt-br|nejˈmaʁ dɐ ˈsiwvɐ ˈsɐ̃tuz‿ˈʒuni.oʁ|audio=Neymar pronunciation.mp3}}<!--This is an IPA transcription based on the actual pronunciation and following [[MOS:PRON]] and [[H:IPA-PT]]. Please do not remove it.-->}} (born 5 February 1992), is a Brazilian professional [[Association football|footballer]] who plays as an [[attacking midfielder]] and a [[Forward (association football)|forward]] for the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]] club [[Santos FC|Santos]], which he also [[Captain (association football)|captains]]. He is known for his dribbling, technical ability, agility, passing, and finishing. Often regarded as one of the greatest players of all time, Neymar is both the [[Brazil national football team#Top goalscorers|all-time top goalscorer]] ([[List of international goals scored by Neymar|80]]) and assist provider (59) for the [[Brazil national football team|Brazil national team]]. He is one of the few players to have scored over 100 goals with three different clubs.{{efn|group=lower-greek|The other four players are [[Isidro Lángara]], [[Romário]], [[Cristiano Ronaldo]], and [[Robert Lewandowski]].}} Official [[Opta Sports|performance data]] ranked him as one of the top two players in Europe for five consecutive seasons from 2015–16 to 2019–20, ranking as the best player in 2016–17 and 2017–18.  

Neymar made his professional debut with Santos in 2009 and soon emerged as a teenage sensation. In [[2011 Santos FC season|2011]], he led Santos to the [[2011 Copa Libertadores|Copa Libertadores]] title and was named the tourname
